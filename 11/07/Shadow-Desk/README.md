# Shadow Desk

Daily Surprise · November 7, 2026

## What it is
A little offline shadow theater. Drag a golden lamp and colored paper cutouts across a tabletop; shadows shift, stretch, and soften as you compose. Save your arrangement as a PNG.

## Privacy-safe inspiration
The general pleasure of making light and geometry tangible. Every shape and starting position is fictional; no personal material was used.

## How to open
Open `index.html` directly in a current browser, including from `file://` offline. No server, install, account, external assets, analytics, upload, or storage. Scene changes last only until the tab closes or reloads; save a PNG if you want to keep one.

## Controls
Drag the golden light or a paper shape with finger, mouse, or stylus. **Add cutout** places another shape (up to twelve on stage; adding beyond that removes the oldest). **Softness** changes the blurred edge of shadows. **Reset scene** asks before discarding your composition. **Save PNG** downloads the stage image (720 × 640 pixels on standard-density screens, up to 1440 × 1280 on high-density screens).

## Why it was built
A tiny tactile instrument where an everyday phenomenon responds to your hands, without needing a camera or a craft drawer.

## Format choice
- **Lane:** `calm_creative_ritual` (creative-coding instrument).
- **Mechanic:** direct manipulation of a lamp and multiple paper cutouts to compose projected shadows, plus adjustable blur and image export. Unlike the recent sentence editor, image ditherer, and pinball game.
