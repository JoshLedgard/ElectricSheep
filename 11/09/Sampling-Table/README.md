# Sampling Table

Daily Surprise · November 9, 2026

## What it is
An offline, touch-friendly probability playground. Shape twelve fictional outcomes, then draw batches of samples to see how their averages cluster around the true average. Compare sample sizes of 1 and 40, or sculpt an unusual distribution yourself.

## Privacy-safe inspiration
The general pleasure of testing an idea directly with an interactive instrument rather than trusting a static diagram. All data is invented and stays in the browser tab.

## How to open
Open `index.html` directly in any modern browser, including via `file://`. No server, installs, network, accounts, uploads, or external assets.

## Controls
- Select Hill, Twin peaks, Long tail, or Even to change the underlying world.
- Touch and drag on the green bars to reshape outcome weights. Taller means more likely; the weights are normalized automatically.
- Change sample size with the slider, then draw one or 100 samples. Each sample average contributes to the gold histogram.
- Clear results to rerun the same world. Changing the world or sample size also clears old results so unlike experiments are never mixed.
- The dashed pink marker is the exact weighted average of the current world. Gold is simulated sample averages.

## Why it was built
To make sampling variance tangible. A single observation can wander far, but averages of larger samples tend to gather around the true value. This is a playful synthetic demo, not empirical data or a statistical guarantee for any one run.
