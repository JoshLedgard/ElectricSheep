# The Cutting Room

Daily Surprise · November 6, 2026

## What it is
An offline, reversible editing desk. Load a short draft, tap sentences to cut or restore them, move sentences within each paragraph, and watch a clean copy emerge. Undo and redo up to 80 edits; copy or download the result.

## Privacy-safe inspiration
Inspired by the general pleasure of making revision tangible. The included sample is invented and contains no personal context.

## How to open
Open `index.html` in a modern browser, including directly through `file://`. It needs no install, server, account, external assets or network. Your draft stays in this tab and is not stored or uploaded.

## Controls
Edit the source and press **Put draft on table**. Tap a sentence to cut or restore it. Use the adjacent ↑ and ↓ buttons to reorder inside that paragraph; use **Undo**, **Redo** or **Restore all** to revise. **Copy clean text** uses the clipboard when available (which may be restricted for local files); **Download .txt** works independently. Rebuilding after edits requests confirmation and replaces the cutting table. Source input is limited to 12,000 characters. Sentence splitting recognizes `.`, `!` and `?` before whitespace; abbreviations may need a source edit and reload.

## Why it was built
A draft often improves when removing words feels reversible instead of destructive. This makes the intermediate state visible while keeping the export clean.

## Format choice
- **Lane:** `writing_or_decision_aid`.
- **Mechanic:** tactile sentence-level cut/restore and within-paragraph reorder, with undo/redo and clean-text export. Not image dithering, arcade physics, or rolling-gear drawing.

## Explicit bans avoided
No field-guide, zine, atlas, almanac or codex framing; no choose-your-own or branching story; no signal/routing/cartographer/constellation puzzle; no launch/readiness simulator, scorecard/rubric meter, abstract AI preflight tool, or generic poetic generator.
