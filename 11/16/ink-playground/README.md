# Ink Playground

**Daily Surprise · November 16, 2026**

## What it is
An offline, tactile letterpress instrument. Set a short phrase as loose movable type, arrange individual letters on a paper sheet, choose inks and pressure, and press multiple passes into a textured print. Undo and redo impressions, clear the sheet, or save your composition as a 1200 × 1600 PNG.

## Inspiration (privacy-safe)
Playful systems and morning curiosity: a small tool that makes experimentation feel physical. No personal conversations or private information appear in the work.

## How to open
Open `index.html` directly in a modern browser, on desktop or phone. No server, login, install, or network connection is needed. Everything stays in this page; nothing is uploaded or saved automatically.

## Controls
1. Enter up to 18 characters and tap **Set type**, or start with the loose `PLAY` letters. **Print a sample** demonstrates overprinted color. Tap a wooden letter block to add another stamp.
2. Drag letters on the paper. Tap one to select it; use the rotate handle/buttons, or pinch and twist with two fingers. Set ink, typeface, size and pressure; when a letter is selected, its type settings apply to that letter, otherwise to all letters.
3. Tap **PRESS** to leave an ink impression. With a letter selected, only it prints; tap empty paper to deselect and press all. Double-tap a letter for a single-letter impression. Repeat with new color or position to overprint.
4. Use **Undo**, **Redo**, **Clear**, **Lift all**, and **Save PNG**. Clear removes ink but keeps loose type. The help button explains the controls in the app.
5. Keyboard: arrow keys nudge the selected letter on the focused sheet; `[ ]` rotate, `N` selects the next letter, `P` prints, Delete removes, and Ctrl/⌘ Z undoes.

## Why it was built
A tiny creative instrument with actual output, rather than another passive prompt or score. The imperfect ink texture and repeatable overprints make small decisions visible.
