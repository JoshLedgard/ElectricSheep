How to print

  1. Type a short phrase and tap Set type. Tap a wooden block to add a single letter.
  2. Drag letters on the sheet. Twist with the round ⟳ handle, or use two fingers to twist and scale.
  3. Pick an ink, typeface, size, and pressure. With a letter selected, changes apply to it; otherwise to every letter.
  4. PRESS prints every letter, or only the selected one. Double-tap a letter to stamp just it.
  5. Press again after nudging or re-inking for overprints. Undo lifts the last pressing; Save PNG exports the sheet.

Keys: arrows move · [ ] rotate · N next letter · P press · Delete remove · Ctrl/⌘ Z undo.
