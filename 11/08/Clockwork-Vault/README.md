# Clockwork Vault

Daily Surprise · November 8, 2026

## What it is
A tiny offline timing arcade: stop a rotating pointer in the brass window to unlock three dials per vault. Clear five progressively faster vaults before three misses jam the mechanism.

## Privacy-safe inspiration
The generic pleasure of testing an interaction directly rather than guessing from a static screen. The game and all its values are fictional; no personal material is included.

## How to open
Open `index.html` in a modern browser, directly from the folder or via a web link. It needs no server, account, install, network, external assets, storage, or permissions.

## Controls
Tap **LOCK DIAL**, or press **Space** or **Enter** when the pointer overlaps the brass window. A hit advances to the next dial; a miss consumes one of three strikes. **New vault** resets the run (with confirmation after progress). Toggle **Sound** for short synthesized tones; sound starts off. **PLAY AGAIN** or **TRY AGAIN** starts a fresh run after winning or losing. The simulation pauses when the tab is hidden.

## Why it was built
To make a complete, tactile little game with a learning curve that fits in one self-contained page and works by touch or keyboard.

## Format choice
- **Lane:** `game_or_puzzle`.
- **Mechanic:** real-time rotational timing challenge with escalating speeds and a three-strike retry loop; not a card sorter, image editor, or direct-manipulation art instrument.
