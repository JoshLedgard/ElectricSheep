The 10‑second tour

A tiny four‑track drum machine. Everything runs in this page: no downloads or sign‑in needed.

  1. 1Tap squares to toggle hits. Each row is a drum, each column a 16th note.
  2. 2Play (or press P) and shape the groove with Tempo, Swing, mute and volume.
  3. 3Try a preset, then Render WAV to download the loop or Copy share link to send the pattern.
