# Pocket Drum Machine

**Daily Surprise · November 15, 2026**

## What it is
A compact, offline four-track drum sequencer. Tap a 16-step pattern for kick, snare, hi-hat, and clap; hear the loop, mix it, and render a real WAV file. Four preset grooves offer a quick starting point. The sound is synthesized in your browser, not streamed or downloaded.

## Inspiration (privacy-safe)
A playful-systems theme: small, tactile tools can make a spare minute creative. No personal conversation, documents, identity, or private data is embedded.

## How to open
Open `index.html` in a modern browser, including directly from this folder. No install, account, server, or network required. On a phone, tap **Play** to unlock audio. If browser audio is unavailable, you can still edit a pattern and try the built-in WAV renderer.

## Controls
- Tap steps to toggle notes; the moving highlight marks the current step. Press **Play/Stop** to hear your groove.
- Select a preset, adjust tempo, swing, track volume or mute, and try Randomize, Clear, and Undo.
- Choose a loop length and **Render WAV**; preview or download the resulting audio. Render again after edits.
- **Share** copies a link to the published ElectricSheep copy with the pattern and mixer settings encoded in its fragment; if clipboard access is blocked, select the displayed link instead. Sharing depends on the published page being live. The pattern fragment is processed locally in the browser and is not sent to the server.
- Keyboard: Tab to controls; P or Space (away from controls) to play/stop; 1–4 mute tracks; ± changes tempo; ? opens help. Step buttons can be activated with Enter/Space.
