# Chroma Room

A tiny private color laboratory: import a photo or play with the built-in abstract samples, inspect six dominant color families, tap pixels with an eyedropper, and copy HEX values or a CSS palette.

**Privacy-safe inspiration:** a curiosity about useful, tactile product interfaces. This is not derived from a private conversation or personal image.

**How to open:** Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No install, account, network connection, or server needed.

**Controls:** Tap **Open an image** to process a PNG/JPEG/WebP locally, or **Try another sample** to cycle sample art. Tap the preview to sample a pixel; tap a swatch to copy its HEX value. Use **Copy HEX** or **Copy CSS palette** to take the results elsewhere. Keyboard users can focus the image and press Enter/Space to sample its center.

**Why it was built:** To make noticing color feel like a practical, playful act. Your images stay on your device.
