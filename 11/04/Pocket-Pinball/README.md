# Pocket Pinball

Daily Surprise · November 4, 2026

## What it is
An offline, one-page pinball arcade. Launch three balls, keep them moving with two responsive flippers, and hit five bumpers for points. The ball has gravity, collisions, slingshot walls and a speed cap; optional synthesized sound adds a little arcade bounce.

## Privacy-safe inspiration
A generic playful product-prototype impulse: turn a small physical interaction into something immediately usable. The artwork and playfield are invented. No personal context appears in the game.

## How to open
Open `index.html` directly in a modern browser, including from `file://` while offline. No account, install, server, external assets, analytics or network calls. Nothing is stored or uploaded.

## Controls
Tap **Launch ball** to start or launch after a drain. Hold **LEFT FLIP** and **RIGHT FLIP**, or use Left/Right arrows or A/D. Space launches; P pauses and resumes during a ball. The game pauses when the tab is hidden; tap **Resume ball** (the former Launch button) or press P to continue. **New game** resets score and balls (with confirmation when active). Sound is off by default and can be enabled with the Sound button. Each bumper awards the point value printed on it; the game ends after three drains.

## Why it was built
To make a complete, tactile pocket arcade instead of another themed page or abstract concept demo.

## Format choice
- **Lane:** `game_or_puzzle`.
- **Mechanic:** real-time physics arcade with two held flippers, launched balls, collision scoring and three-ball replay. Different from recent painting, masking, rolling-gear drawing and cohort-grid editing.
