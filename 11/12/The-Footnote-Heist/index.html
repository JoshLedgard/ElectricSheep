      How to play & controls

      Each case gives you 2–3 receipts (the only evidence that counts) and an answer split into sentences. Exactly one sentence is either not supported by any receipt or contradicts one. Its little footnote is a decoy — check what the receipt actually says.

      Touch/mouse: tap a sentence, then tap Stamp it unsupported. Keyboard: 1–4 pick a sentence, Enter stamps it, H hint, N next case. Tab works everywhere too.

      Scoring: 100 per case, +25 for each case of current streak beyond the first, −50 if you used a hint. Everything here is invented; nothing describes real places, people or facts.

      All case files are fiction. No data leaves this page.
