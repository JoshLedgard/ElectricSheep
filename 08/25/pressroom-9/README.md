# Pressroom 9 — A Tactile Letterpress Instrument

*Daily Surprise · 2026-08-25*

## What it is

Pressroom 9 is a self-contained, fictional pocket letterpress. Set a short phrase, choose an ink, roll directly across the type, hold the platen until it locks, and peel back the paper to reveal a proof. Each pull preserves subtle deterministic differences in ink coverage and pressure. Up to four proofs can hang on the drying rack, and the selected proof can be exported as a genuine local SVG.

This is a hands-on creative instrument rather than a random phrase or poster generator: the result follows the words, ink, pressure, and rolling gesture you provide.

Everything runs in the current browser tab. There are no dependencies, external assets, network requests, accounts, analytics, persistence, uploads, camera, microphone, or sound.

## How to open

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. It works directly from `file://` and needs no server or installation.

## Controls

1. Edit the short phrase in **Type drawer**.
2. Adjust **Ink load** and **Pressure**, then select an ink tin.
3. Tap **Load roller**.
4. Drag or swipe back and forth directly over the paper. The ink meter grows with distance traveled.
5. When enough ink has accumulated, press and hold **Hold to press** until the platen locks.
6. Tap **Peel print** for the reveal.
7. Pull up to four proofs, select one on the drying rack, and use **Export SVG** to save it locally.

The core controls work with touch, pointer, or keyboard. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

## Why it was built

A tiny physical-feeling craft can turn iteration into play. Pressroom 9 makes each revision tangible: wording becomes movable type, effort becomes ink coverage, commitment becomes pressure, and review becomes a drying rack of small differences. It is meant to feel like a future creative app that somehow contains a heavy cast-iron machine.
