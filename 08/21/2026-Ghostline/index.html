How to rehearse

1. Hold the pad at the bottom and slide your thumb up. The further from centre, the faster the script climbs; pull below centre to run it backwards. Release and it stops — unless Cruise is on.
2. Tap any word to make it an anchor. Anchors are the phrases you want to survive.
3. Each time you reach the end, the pass advances and more non-anchor words fade. By pass 4 only your anchors remain lit — say the rest from memory.

Stages

1 Full — every word visible.
2 Thinning — short connective words dim.
3 Ghosted — most of the script is a faint outline.
4 Anchors — only your marked phrases stay lit.
