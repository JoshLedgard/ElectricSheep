Perception arcade · unit 07

Two panels. One difference.

A fictional instrument screen flickers between version A and version B. Exactly one element changes. Tap it.

  • 1Watch the flicker. The blank gap between frames is what makes this hard.
  • 2Tap the thing that changes. Three tries per round; a wrong tap costs 2 seconds.
  • 312 rounds, each subtler than the last. Beat the clock, build a streak.

A toy, not a test. Flickercheck measures nothing about your eyes, brain, or attention — it just hides one change in a picture and dares you to find it. Every panel here is invented.
