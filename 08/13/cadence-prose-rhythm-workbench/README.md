# Cadence — A Prose Rhythm Workbench

*Daily Surprise · 2026-08-13*

## What it is

Cadence is a self-contained writing instrument that makes sentence rhythm visible and audible. Edit the loaded fictional passage—or paste your own prose—and each sentence becomes a proportional beat whose width reflects length and whose marks reveal punctuation. Tap a beat to focus the matching sentence, pin an original draft, revise it, and compare the two contours without turning the writing into a grade.

A gesture-triggered Web Audio audition plays a low pulse for each sentence and light ticks for clause breaks. It uses synthesis only: no speech, microphone, recording, or audio files. The revised text can be copied or downloaded as a real `.txt` file.

## Privacy-safe inspiration

The generic inspiration was the craft of polishing presentation details and making complex material easier to inspect. That became a local workbench for feeling prose cadence. Cadence contains no real people, organizations, projects, conversations, support content, analytics, or private source material. Its built-in passage is wholly fictional and neutral.

Everything runs inside the current browser tab. There are no accounts, uploads, analytics, network requests, external assets, saved browser data, microphone, packages, or server.

## How to open

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No installation, account, internet connection, or server is required.

## Controls

- Edit or paste prose in the editor; the rhythm strip and contour update immediately.
- Tap a sentence beat to select and focus its corresponding prose.
- Use **Pin as draft A**, revise the text, then toggle **Compare A/B** to inspect the original and revision.
- Use **Play rhythm**, **Stop**, and **Mute** for the optional synthesized percussion audition.
- Use **Copy text** or **Download .txt** to take the actual revision with you.
- Use **Load sample** to restore the harmless fictional passage.

## Why it was built

Writing tools often count words or judge prose. Cadence instead gives editing a tactile, temporal surface: it helps a writer notice long runs, short interruptions, and punctuation patterns while keeping every decision human. It is a small but real-feeling local app rather than another themed webpage.

## Format choice

- **Lane:** `writing_or_decision_aid` — presented as a polished, real-feeling local writing app.
- **Mechanic:** synchronized inline prose editing plus a tappable sentence-rhythm timeline, optional non-speech Web Audio percussion audition, and an A/B draft snapshot with real copy/download output.

This lane and mechanic differ from the last three surprises' responsive-layout studio, one-thumb rhythm arcade game, and kinetic-type performance instrument.

## Explicit bans avoided

Cadence is not a field guide, zine, atlas, almanac, codex, bestiary, or pocket guide; not a choose-your-own, branching story, microquest, or multiple-ending experience; not a signal, cartography, constellation, routing, or path-connection metaphor; not a launch, readiness, shipping, scorecard, rubric, meter, grading, preflight, or abstract AI-agent context tool; and not a poetic generator. It also avoids family/travel planning, baseball, and local-utility framing.

## Privacy check

- No real names, organizations, emails, addresses, financial data, credentials, private quotations, or personal analytics are included.
- The sample copy is fictional and neutral.
- User-entered text remains in the open browser tab and is processed locally.
- Audio is synthesized only after a user gesture and never records or transmits anything.
