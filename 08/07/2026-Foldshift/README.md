# Foldshift — The Impossible Flatpack Desk

*Daily Surprise · 2026-08-07*

## What it is

Foldshift is a self-contained tactile browser game about making three cheerfully impossible desk objects occupy less reality. Drag the three chunky fold tabs to collapse a Mothlamp, Sidecar, and ceremonial square Teapot into their ghost silhouettes. Each specimen has forgiving folds, a compact transformation, and a final desk tableau after all three are packed.

There is no score, grade, timer, failure state, or generated content. A complete specimen is ready when the page opens, and the three-object loop is designed for a short two-to-three-minute play.

## Privacy-safe inspiration

The generic inspiration was the satisfying work of coordinating changes across several surfaces until a complicated thing fits into a coherent shape, mixed with fresh-start, morning-curiosity, and playful-systems energy. Foldshift contains no real people, organizations, projects, accounts, locations, conversations, messages, or datasets.

Everything runs locally in the open browser tab. There are no accounts, uploads, analytics, network requests, external assets, saved browser data, packages, or server. Optional synthesized clicks are created locally only after sound is enabled.

## How to open

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No setup, connection, installation, or server is required.

## Controls

- Drag each of the three large fold tabs from left to right.
- Use **Left/Right** or **Up/Down** arrow keys while a tab is focused.
- Pack all three folds to unlock the next specimen.
- **Flatten this one** resets the current specimen.
- The circular music control enables or disables optional locally synthesized sound; sound begins off.
- On a phone or tablet, all primary controls work with touch and do not depend on hover.

## Why it was built

This is a deliberately physical-feeling change of pace: a tiny geometric toy that treats compactness as delight rather than optimization. It is meant to feel like a polished fictional app discovered on an unusual desk, not a decorated webpage.

## Format choice

- **Lane:** `game_or_puzzle` — a weird playable game presented as a polished fictional future app.
- **Mechanic:** direct crease-folding and silhouette assembly across three deterministic specimens. The player drags chunky fold tabs, making connected illustrated panels rotate, compress, and settle into a target. This differs from the last three surprises' spoken rhythm capture, temporal scene scrubbing, and two-state interface choreography.
