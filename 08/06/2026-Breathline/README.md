# Breathline — A Spoken-Draft Rehearsal Desk

*Daily Surprise · 2026-08-06*

## What it is

Breathline is a self-contained writing and speaking instrument that preserves the human cadence of a short draft. Bring a draft, begin a rehearsal, speak each highlighted thought aloud, and tap when the thought lands. Breathline records only the elapsed time between taps—never audio—and turns that rhythm into a typographic teleprompter and factual cue sheet.

A harmless fictional demonstration is loaded at opening, so the complete draft → rehearse → play → export loop is ready immediately. Playback tempo can change without flattening the relative pacing of the take. The resulting cue sheet exports as plain text, while the rehearsal exports as a standalone dependency-free HTML player.

Everything runs locally in the open browser tab. There is no microphone permission, network request, account, upload, analytics, external asset, package, server, or saved personal content.

## Privacy-safe inspiration

The generic inspiration was the challenge of making complex work easier to communicate across software surfaces while preserving the human rhythm of an idea. Breathline contains no real people, organizations, repositories, projects, conversations, messages, addresses, accounts, or private data. Its built-in copy is wholly fictional.

## How to open

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No setup or server is required.

## Controls

1. **Draft:** Edit or replace the built-in sample, then choose **Prepare rehearsal**.
2. **Rehearse:** Press the large start control, speak the highlighted phrase, and tap when that thought lands. Repeat until the take is complete. No microphone is used.
3. **Play:** Play the typographic take, restart it, or adjust tempo from 70% to 140%.
4. **Take:** Review the factual timing receipt and download a plain-text cue sheet or a standalone rehearsal HTML file. Clipboard copy is available where the browser permits it.
5. The workflow tabs remain available throughout. Controls are designed for touch, pointer, and keyboard use.

## Why it was built

Text often loses the rests, emphasis, and variable pace that make an explanation feel human. Breathline captures those choices with one simple physical gesture and turns them into a portable artifact—without recording a voice or evaluating a performance.

## Format choice

- **Lane:** `writing_or_decision_aid` — presented as a polished future artifact and believable local creative tool.
- **Mechanic:** edit a draft → speak highlighted thought units → tap one large control at naturally felt boundaries → capture interval rhythm without audio → replay a continuously paced typographic rehearsal → export a real cue sheet or standalone HTML player. This direct perform-and-replay interaction differs from the last three surprises' temporal scene scrubbing, two-state interface choreography, and diff-to-textile inspection.

## Explicit bans avoided

Breathline is not a field guide, atlas, almanac, codex, bestiary, zine, choose-your-own or branching story, microquest, signal-routing/cartographer/constellation puzzle, launch/readiness/shipping simulator, scorecard, rubric meter, AI-agent preflight/context debugger, generic generator, card board, map explorer, or visual story/comic. It does not score, grade, transcribe, rewrite, or generate prose.

## Privacy and accessibility

- No real personal or organizational details are included.
- User text and timings remain only in the current browser tab unless the user explicitly exports them.
- There are no network calls or persistent browser storage.
- Touch targets are at least 44px, the layout fits a 390px viewport, core controls are keyboard operable, and reduced-motion preferences disable transitions.
