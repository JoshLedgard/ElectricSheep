# Counterweight — A Kinetic Mobile Workbench

*Daily Surprise · 2026-08-01*

## What it is

Counterweight is a self-contained kinetic sculpture workbench. Four abstract forms hang from a three-pivot mobile. Slide any form sideways along its arm and the complete structure tilts, swings, and settles in response. Give it a breath, remix the arrangement, shift the room light, and save the sculpture as a real SVG.

A composed mobile is ready when the page opens, so the drag → disturb → settle → export loop is immediately playable. The physics are deliberately gentle and illustrative rather than an engineering simulation. Nothing is scored, graded, generated from prose, uploaded, or saved remotely.

## Privacy-safe inspiration

The generic inspiration was the tactile pleasure of coordinating changes across several connected surfaces and seeing a small adjustment reshape the whole. That broad interaction-design theme became a harmless hanging sculpture. Counterweight contains no real people, organizations, projects, accounts, conversations, messages, places, or datasets.

Everything runs locally in the open browser tab. There are no accounts, uploads, analytics, network requests, external assets, packages, or persistent browser storage.

## How to open

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No server, installation, account, or internet connection is required.

## Controls

- **Drag a form sideways** to move its attachment point along the lower arm. The other pivots respond and settle.
- **Give a breath** to set all three pivots gently moving.
- **Remix** to hang a fresh deterministic-feeling arrangement of the same four forms.
- Use the three **light controls** to switch between dawn, room, and blue illumination.
- **Keep SVG** downloads the current sculpture as a standalone vector image.
- Reduced-motion browser preferences are respected by settling the structure immediately.

## Why it was built

A useful interface does not always need to explain, grade, or optimize. Counterweight explores a different kind of feedback: direct manipulation, visible consequence, and enough quiet motion to make balance feel tangible. It is meant to be a polished two-minute creative instrument rather than a themed webpage.

## Format choice

- **Mechanic:** direct-manipulation kinetic sculpture. Slide physical-looking weights along linked hanging arms, disturb the structure with a breath, watch a lightweight hierarchical physics model tilt and settle, adjust illumination, and export the live arrangement as SVG.
