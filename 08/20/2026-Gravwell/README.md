# Gravwell — A Pocket Sand Table for Restless Minutes

*Daily Surprise · 2026-08-20*

## What it is

Gravwell is a self-contained kinetic sand table disguised as a strange, high-end pocket device. Thousands of glowing grains drift through a dark ceramic well, settle around physical stones, and respond to a movable gravity puck. On supported phones, the whole table can also answer to device tilt.

It opens already alive: no setup, account, server, or source material is required. Shape the current for a minute or two, place a few obstacles, let the grains settle, then **Fossilize** the result as a genuine local PNG.

Everything is rendered and simulated in the current browser tab. Gravwell has no external assets, network requests, analytics, account, persistence, upload, microphone, or camera.

## Privacy-safe inspiration

The broad inspiration was the generic rhythm of project tracking, operational sweeps, and careful follow-through—and the value of a small tactile pause between focused tasks. That theme became a wholly fictional decompression object. It does not use or refer to any real person, organization, project, company, customer, account, message, conversation, or source data.

## How to open

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. It works directly from `file://` and requires no installation, package, internet connection, or local server.

For the richest mobile interaction, open it on a phone and choose **Enable tilt**. Browsers that require motion permission request it only after that tap. The on-screen gravity puck remains a full pointer/touch fallback.

## Controls

- **Drag the gravity puck** to pull the current through the sand.
- **Enable tilt** on a supported phone to steer by gently tipping the device.
- **Tap the well** to place a physical stone that bends the flow.
- **Press and hold** in the well to gently erase nearby grains or a placed stone.
- Use **Pause** when you want the table to hold still.
- Use **Undo** or **Reset** to recover and begin a fresh composition.
- Toggle the **speaker** for optional synthesized tactile sound after a gesture.
- Choose **Fossilize** to export the current table as a flattened PNG with its title and date embedded.

The interface is mobile-first, uses large touch targets, provides pointer controls for desktop, and respects reduced-motion preferences.

## Why it was built

To make an overnight artifact that feels less like a themed webpage and more like a device that should exist: a tiny, authored physical simulation with enough depth for a satisfying two- or three-minute ritual and a real keepsake at the end.

## Format choice

- **Lane:** `calm_creative_ritual` — presented as a polished future artifact / fake pocket device.
- **Mechanic:** device-tilt or drag-to-steer particle physics. A gravity puck moves granular sand around tappable physical stones; press-and-hold erases, settling creates the temporal arc, and Fossilize exports the final state as a genuine PNG. It is a directly played instrument, not a random generator.

This differs from the previous three surprises’ ergonomic target manipulation, demand-curve and price-rail drawing, and cinematic scroll-and-peel interaction.
