# Chronosnatch — Steal Back 60 Seconds

## What it is

A self-contained one-thumb rhythm game disguised as an eccentric retro-future watch heist. Catch the sweeping hand inside illuminated capture windows, chain clean hits, and steal a complete minute before the museum clock catches you.

## Privacy-safe inspiration

Inspired by the generic idea that recurring rituals and small pieces of time can feel more alive when turned into play. It contains no personal source material, quotations, accounts, analytics, or network requests.

## How to open

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No server, installation, account, or internet connection is required.

## Controls

- Tap **STEAL SECOND**, tap the clock face, press **Space**, or press **Enter** when the hand crosses the glowing capture window.
- Precise hits steal more seconds; every five-hit combo pays a bonus.
- Three misses trigger the alarm.
- Use the speaker button to mute or unmute synthesized sound.
- Use **Restart** at any time for a fresh run.

## Why it was built

To make a tiny, polished arcade object with a complete two-minute game arc rather than another themed information page. It is designed for one thumb but works equally well with mouse and keyboard.

## Format choice

- **Lane:** weird playable game
- **Mechanic:** one-thumb rhythm/timing arcade play—intercept a sweeping clock hand inside moving angular windows to accumulate 60 stolen seconds.
