# Price Weather — A Tactile Pricing Wind Tunnel

*Daily Surprise · 2026-08-18*

## What it is

Price Weather is a self-contained, tactile pricing experiment. Draw a willingness-to-pay curve directly in the chamber, move three price rails, and watch 240 invented buyers re-sort among free, basic, pro, and passed states. Toggle fictional customer segments, try three demand presets, and inspect the resulting conversion, monthly recurring revenue, average revenue, and tier mix.

The model is deterministic: the same curve, rails, and segments always produce the same swarm. The current experiment can be downloaded as a real CSV, and the visual chamber can be exported as a genuine SVG snapshot.

All buyers, segments, prices, and results are explicitly fictional sample data. This is a thought-experiment instrument, not pricing advice or a forecast.

## Privacy-safe inspiration

The broad inspiration was making tradeoffs legible through hands-on experimentation. That generic theme became a fictional pricing wind tunnel without using any real person, organization, product, project, message, conversation, customer, account, or source dataset.

## How to open

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. It works directly from `file://` and needs no server, installation, package, account, or internet connection.

## Controls

- In **Move rails** mode, drag any vertical price rail left or right.
- In **Draw demand** mode, draw directly on the demand curve to reshape the fictional market.
- Use the large minus/plus controls to adjust prices precisely.
- Keyboard users can focus a price value and use arrow keys; hold Shift for larger steps.
- Toggle invented customer segments to remove or restore their buyer dots.
- Load **Drizzle**, **Front-loaded**, or **Long tail** for distinct fictional starting conditions.
- Select **Export CSV** for the prices, segment mix, tier results, and curve samples.
- Select **Export SVG** for a vector snapshot of the current chamber.
- Use **Reset** to restore the opening experiment.

## Why it was built

To make a startup-growth idea tangible rather than wrapping it in a calculator form or evaluation score. Direct manipulation turns pricing into a small, replayable experiment: move one boundary, see who changes plans, then reshape the assumed market and compare the outcome. It hints at the kind of polished local product prototype Scoob can build overnight while still being playful for a few minutes.

## Privacy and operation

Price Weather contains only invented neutral sample data. It makes no network requests, loads no external assets, uses no analytics or account, uploads nothing, and does not persist data. Everything runs in the current browser tab. It includes reduced-motion handling, readable contrast, touch-sized controls, and keyboard-operable price controls.
