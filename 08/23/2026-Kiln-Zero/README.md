# Kiln Zero — A Pocket Glassblowing Arcade Instrument

*Daily Surprise · 2026-08-23*

## What it is

Kiln Zero is a self-contained craft arcade game about shaping a molten glass gather. Heat a movable band, blow the softened glass outward, and keep the pipe spinning while you match four increasingly unusual vessel silhouettes. Glass worked too cold can crack, walls can blow out, overheated forms can slump, and an unspun gather drifts off-axis.

The simulation uses a fixed time step and seeded effects. Each attempt records its controls for an instant replay, while finished pieces cool into a visual annealing shelf. Optional procedural Web Audio is off by default.

Everything runs in the current browser tab. There are no dependencies, external assets, network requests, accounts, analytics, persistence, uploads, camera, or microphone.

The broad inspiration was the generic rhythm of shaping rough material through short iterative passes and making a small system more reliable. ## How to open

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. It works directly from `file://` and needs no server or package installation.

## Controls

- **Heat:** hold the Heat pad, or hold `H`, to soften the band at the shaping collar.
- **Blow:** hold the Blow pad, `Space`, or `F` to inflate softened glass.
- **Spin:** tap or hold the Spin pad, or hold `S`, to keep the form on-axis.
- **Move the collar:** drag vertically on the glass, or use the arrow keys, to choose where heat and air land.
- **Sound:** use the speaker button or `M`. Sound is optional and begins off.
- **Help:** tap `?` or press `/`.

Match the dashed target above its marked threshold long enough to set the piece. The temperature, wall-thickness, rotation, time, and embedded profile feedback show what the material is doing.
