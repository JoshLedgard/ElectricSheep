# Tapprint — A One-Handed Interface Reach Lab

*Daily Surprise · 2026-08-19*

## What it is

Tapprint is a self-contained browser workbench for exploring one-handed mobile interface reach. A harmless fictional phone specimen is ready at startup. Move the thumb origin, switch hands, change the simulated phone size, place and drag action targets, and run a deterministic reach sweep to see how a simple geometric thumb model meets the layout.

You can also import, paste, or drop a screenshot for local inspection and export a flattened annotated PNG containing the specimen, reach visualization, and target annotations. Imported images remain in the current browser tab.

The visualization is an ergonomic thought experiment for sketching layout intent. It is not medical advice, a model of any real hand, or accessibility-compliance guidance; real testing with people still matters.

## Privacy-safe inspiration

The broad inspiration was a generic interest in rich interactive HTML prototypes and polishing product interactions. That theme became an invented local design instrument without using any real person, organization, product, project, customer, message, conversation, account, or source screenshot.

## How to open

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No server, package installation, account, or internet connection is needed.

## Controls

- Drag the glowing thumb-origin control on the phone specimen.
- Switch between left- and right-hand grip.
- Adjust the simulated phone size.
- Add targets, then drag them directly on the specimen; focused targets also support keyboard movement.
- Run the reach sweep to animate the deterministic geometric model.
- Import, paste, or drop an image to inspect your own local specimen.
- Export a flattened annotated PNG, or reset the bench to its fictional starter state.

## Why it was built

To turn a subtle interface-design question into a tactile object: not a report or grade, but a small lab where spatial assumptions can be moved, felt, and exported. It is meant to hint at the kind of useful, polished product-design tooling Scoob can dream up overnight.

## Format choice

- **Lane:** `practical_micro_tool` — presented as a polished future artifact and real-feeling local design app.
- **Mechanic:** direct touch/pointer manipulation of a thumb origin and action targets on a phone canvas, plus grip switching, phone-size scrubbing, a deterministic one-handed reach sweep, local screenshot import, and genuine flattened PNG export.
