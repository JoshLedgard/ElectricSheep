Drag the amber puck to move the thumb base. Drag any ring to move a target. With a target focused, arrow keys nudge it (hold Shift for coarse), Delete removes it.

What this is not: reach pressure here comes from a simple geometric thumb model — a pivot, an arc, and an angular comfort span. It is an ergonomic thought experiment for sketching layout intent, not medical advice, not a claim about any real hand, and not accessibility-compliance guidance. Real hands rotate, slide, and regrip constantly. Test with people.
