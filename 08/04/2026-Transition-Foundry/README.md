# Transition Foundry — A Two-State Interface Choreographer

*Daily Surprise · 2026-08-04*

## What it is

Transition Foundry is a self-contained product-design instrument for staging two compact interface states and working directly with the motion between them. A harmless fictional invitation-to-workspace transition is loaded on opening. Drag its four matched elements in either state, tune their labels, geometry, color, corner shape, and presence, then scrub or play the resulting interpolation.

The useful artifact is not a screenshot: the Foundry exports the current choreography as a dependency-free HTML prototype with its own scrubber and playback control. It can also export or copy a readable JSON motion recipe.

## Privacy-safe inspiration

The generic inspiration was the craft of coordinating software changes across multiple work surfaces while keeping shared intent coherent. That broad product-design theme became a local two-state motion workbench. Transition Foundry contains no real people, organizations, repositories, conversations, messages, accounts, or project data.

Anything edited stays only in the open browser tab. There are no accounts, uploads, analytics, network requests, external assets, saved browser data, packages, or server.

## How to open

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No server, install, account, or network connection is required.

## Controls

- **Drag an element** in the BEFORE or AFTER stage to reposition it.
- **Tap an element** to select it, then use the inspector to edit its label, X/Y position, width, height, color, corner radius, and presence.
- **Keyboard:** focus an element and use the arrow keys to nudge it; hold Shift for a larger step.
- **Scrub** the transition slider to inspect continuity at any point.
- Choose a motion curve, tune duration, and press **Play** to watch the complete choreography.
- **Export standalone HTML** downloads a working offline transition prototype containing the exact current states.
- Export or copy the **motion JSON** for a readable recipe.
- **Reset demo** restores the original fictional composition.

## Why it was built

Motion design often begins after two static screens already exist, which makes the most important question easy to miss: what remains continuous between them? This little instrument makes that continuity directly editable and gives the experiment a portable output. It is meant to feel like a believable tool from a product studio rather than a themed webpage.
