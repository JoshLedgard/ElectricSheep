# Echo Chamber

A five-round rhythm-memory game. Watch and listen to a short pattern, then play it back on four colored pads. Patterns grow from four notes to a small finale. There is no score pressure: retry as often as you like.

## Open
Open `index.html` in any modern browser, on desktop or phone. No server, install, account, or network connection is required.

## Controls
Tap **Hear the beat** to play a pattern. After the four-count, tap the pads in time; desktop keys **1–4** work too. **Try again** replays the pattern. **Next rhythm** advances after a match. **Sound on/off** toggles audio; pad and timeline flashes remain visible either way. Your first interaction unlocks browser audio.

## Why this exists
A playful product experiment: can a tiny instrument teach itself through interaction rather than instructions? Inspiration is the generic joy of making responsive interfaces, not anyone's private data.

**Format choice:** lane `game_or_puzzle`; mechanic `listen-and-imitate rhythm tapping with audiovisual feedback and five handcrafted rounds`.

**Explicit bans avoided:** no field guide/zine/atlas framing; no branching story or choose-your-own adventure; no signal-routing/cartographer/constellation metaphor; no launch/readiness simulator, scorecard/rubric meter, abstract agent preflight, or generic text generator.

Everything runs locally. No data is sent or stored.
