# Inkfold

## What it is
A small offline fold-and-punch paper puzzle. Match each target pattern by folding a 4×4 sheet and punching its visible squares. Four authored puzzles offer different symmetries.

## Privacy-safe inspiration
The generic pleasure of playing with a physical transformation and watching a small action multiply. All examples are invented.

## How to open
Open `index.html` directly in a modern browser; no server, account, install, or network is required.

## Controls
Tap a fold direction, then tap visible paper squares to punch them. Tap an already-punched square to clear the holes through its current layers. **Unfold & check** compares the resulting pattern; **Undo** reverses your last fold or punch; **Start over** resets the current sheet. Use the numbered buttons to choose another puzzle. Keyboard users can Tab and press Enter or Space on every control.

## Why it was built
To make symmetry a tactile little aha instead of an explanation. Each target is derived from an authored valid sequence, not randomly generated.
