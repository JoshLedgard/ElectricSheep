# Pocket Drum Machine

Daily Surprise · October 26, 2026

## What it is
An offline four-voice, sixteen-step drum sequencer. Compose a loop by touching its grid, play the beat, adjust tempo and swing, then download a four-bar PCM WAV file.

## Privacy-safe inspiration
Inspired by the simple pleasure of making and taking home a small creative artifact. No personal context or private data is included.

## How to open
Open `index.html` directly in a current browser, including from `file://`. No server, installation, account, samples, or network connection needed. Audio needs a tap on Play to start. On devices with a mute switch, ensure sound is enabled.

## Controls
Tap a square to toggle one of four drums at that sixteenth-note step. Play/Stop starts and stops the loop; the white outline shows the current step. Change Tempo (60–180 BPM) and Swing (0–55%). Classic beat restores the starting pattern. Surprise beat makes a fresh random rhythm you can edit. Clear asks before deleting the pattern. Download 4-bar WAV saves four repetitions of the current beat; export works without pressing Play. The grid is navigable by keyboard and squares announce their voice, step number, and pressed state.

## Why it was built
A small instrument is more fun when the result is tangible: the WAV can be brought into a larger project. Sound is synthesized locally, rather than fetched from a sample library.
