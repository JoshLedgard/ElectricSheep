Cut Room

Paste a draft. It becomes a strip of sentence cards you can move, cut and restore. Then compare what you had with what you made.

Stays on this device, in memory only. Nothing is saved or sent.

Sentence cards

Each card shows where it started (“was #3”) and a length bar — red bars are long sentences worth a second look. ¶ marks a sentence that opens a paragraph. Tip: with focus on a card, Alt+↑/↓ moves it.
