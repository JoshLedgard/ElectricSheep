# Cut Room

A pocket-sized sentence editing bench: paste a draft, cut it into cards, rearrange or remove sentences, then compare and copy the tighter version. Three synthetic samples offer a quick challenge: bring the important sentence forward.

**Inspiration:** A generic interest in interfaces that let people see and change a piece of writing rather than merely receive advice. No private conversation or personal material was used.

**Open:** Open `index.html` in any modern browser, including from the downloaded folder on a phone or desktop. No server, installation, account, or network required.

**Controls:** Paste text or tap **Try a sample**, then **Cut into cards**. Each card has **Up**, **Down**, a **¶** paragraph-break toggle, and **Cut**; open the cutting-room floor to **Restore** removed sentences. Use **Undo**, **Reset**, the before/after tabs, and **Copy edit**. Keyboard: focus a card and press Alt+Up/Down to move; Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes. Up to 20,000 characters and 200 cards. If clipboard access is blocked, select and copy the Edited preview manually.

**Why:** Editing is often easier when sentences become physical-feeling pieces that can move. This tool keeps the original visible and makes cuts reversible.

**Format choice:** lane: `writing_or_decision_aid`; mechanic: `reorder/cut/restore sentence cards with live before-and-after preview and undo`.

**Explicit bans avoided:** No field guide, zine, atlas, almanac or codex; no branching story or choose-your-own adventure; no signal-routing/cartographer/constellation metaphor; no launch/readiness simulator, scorecard/rubric meter, abstract agent preflight, or generic generator.

Your input remains in this browser tab's memory. Nothing is stored or sent.
