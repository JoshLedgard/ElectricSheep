Flipbook Desk

Draw one moment, duplicate it, nudge the next. Tap Play to watch your little motion come alive. A bouncing dot is waiting as an example—replace it with anything.

The pale ghost shows the previous frame. Eraser removes pixels, not the white background. Your art stays in this tab until you download it.

Frames

Export arranges frames in one horizontal strip. Each frame is 320 × 240 pixels; transparent artwork stays transparent.

Tip: duplicate a frame before changing it. To start from scratch, clear each example frame or delete them, then draw. Maximum 24 frames.
