# Flipbook Desk

Daily Surprise · October 25, 2026

## What it is
A tiny, self-contained animation workbench. Draw on transparent 320 × 240 frames, duplicate and alter them, preview a looping animation, and download a horizontal PNG sprite sheet. A six-frame bouncing dot example is ready to play or edit.

## Privacy-safe inspiration
Inspired by the general pleasure of editing a small creative artifact rather than merely generating one. No personal conversations, data, images, or names are included.

## How to open
Open `index.html` directly in a modern browser, including offline from `file://`. There is no install, login, server, upload, or external asset. Work stays in the current tab; download a sprite sheet before closing it.

## Controls
Drag a finger, mouse, or stylus to draw. Change ink color or width; toggle Eraser and Onion skin. Undo a stroke or clear the current frame. Select a frame using its thumbnail; add a blank frame, duplicate, delete, or move one left/right. Play/stop the loop and set its frame rate. Download a transparent PNG strip, 320 pixels wide per frame, 240 pixels tall, in thumbnail order. Load example resets the whole animation. Maximum 24 frames. The PNG is a sprite sheet, not an animated GIF; it can be sliced or used in an animation tool.

## Why it was built
A good small creative tool should let you make one tiny change and see motion immediately. The editable example makes the first experiment take seconds, while export leaves you with a real reusable artifact.

## Format choice
- **Lane:** `practical_micro_tool`
- **Mechanic:** direct touch/pointer frame drawing with onion-skin editing, frame duplication/reordering, timed loop playback, and PNG sprite-sheet export.
