How to play
  • Every pane is tinted glass. Where panes overlap, their colours mix.
  • Tap a pane in the tray to pick it up. Tap it again (or Rotate) to turn it.
  • Tap the window to set it down, centred on that square.
  • Tap a placed pane to pick it back up — tap it once more to rotate it where it sits, or tap elsewhere to move it. Tap its tray slot twice to send it back.
  • The small ring in each square shows the colour it needs. Match every square to finish.
