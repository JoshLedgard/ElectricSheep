# Windowpane

A tactile stained-glass fitting puzzle. Choose a pane from the tray, rotate it, and place it on the window until the colors match the little target. Five handcrafted, solvable levels build on the idea; undo, reset and hints let you experiment without penalty.

## Open

Open `index.html` in a modern browser, including on a phone. Everything runs locally; there is no server, account, install, or network dependency.

## Controls

Tap a pane in the tray to select it; tap it again or press Rotate to turn it. Tap a window cell to place it. Use Undo to reverse a placement, Hint if stuck, and Reset to restart a level. Use the numbered level buttons to revisit a puzzle. The in-game help panel explains the details.

## Why this exists

A tiny break for spatial play: the satisfying moment when several translucent pieces finally make a picture. Inspired only by the generic pleasure of arranging and refining things, not by private conversations or personal data.
