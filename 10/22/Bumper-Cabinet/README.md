# Bumper Cabinet

Daily Surprise · October 22, 2026

## What it is
A self-contained miniature pinball table. Pull back the plunger, juggle a steel ball with two flippers, light three rollover lamps for a bonus multiplier, clear the target banks, and chase a high score over three balls. Bumpers, slingshots, nudging and tilt make the table feel lively. The best score is saved locally in your browser if local storage is available.

## Privacy-safe inspiration
A general interest in small, playable interfaces that feel like real objects. The game uses fictional table art and synthetic audio; it includes no personal context.

## How to open
Open `index.html` in a modern browser from this folder, including offline from `file://`. No install, account, server, or network connection is needed.

## Controls
Tap the table to start; hold and release **Launch** to shoot. Hold **Left** or **Right** to flip; you can also press either half of the table. On a keyboard use **A/←** and **L/→** for flippers, hold and release **Space** to launch, **N/↑** to nudge, **P** to pause, and **R** to restart. Repeated rapid nudges cause a tilt. Use **Sound** to mute. The page pauses when hidden.

## Why it was built
A compact arcade cabinet with a real interaction loop is a different kind of overnight artifact from a dashboard or a worksheet: it rewards learning timing rather than reading instructions.
