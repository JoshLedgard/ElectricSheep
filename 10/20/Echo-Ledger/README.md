# Echo Ledger

Daily Surprise · October 20, 2026

## What it is
An offline, side-by-side thought experiment for referral growth. Set fictional starting signups, share participation, invites per sharer, invite conversion, and the delay to conversion. It calculates day-by-day expected new signups and a thirty-day cumulative total for each scenario. The chart and expandable table show the trajectory; CSV export keeps the numbers accessible. These are mathematical expectations, not observed results or forecasts.

## Privacy-safe inspiration
Inspired by the general pleasure of making a product hypothesis tangible and testing which assumption matters. No personal context or real campaign data is included.

## How to open
Open `index.html` in a modern browser, including directly from the file system. It needs no server, install, login, or internet access.

## Controls
Edit any number in Baseline or Experiment. Results update immediately. **Reset example** restores the fictional starting assumptions. **Download daily CSV** exports all 30 days. Open **Daily values and calculation** for the full table and formula. All fields and controls work with touch and keyboard.

## Why it was built
A compact way to feel the time delay and compounding of a referral loop without hiding the math behind a single score or pretending hypothetical inputs are evidence.

## Format choice
- **Lane:** `startup_growth_experiment`
- **Mechanic:** editable side-by-side numeric hypotheses with deterministic day-by-day cohort replay, paired bar comparison, table, and CSV export. No dial tuning or card sorting.
