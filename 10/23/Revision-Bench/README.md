# Revision Bench

Daily Surprise · October 23, 2026

## What it is
An offline, two-draft writing workbench. Paste or type alternate passages, then choose the A or B version of each highlighted word-level change to assemble a final draft. The final text can also be edited directly, copied, or downloaded as plain text. A fictional tide-clock passage loads as an example.

## Privacy-safe inspiration
Inspired by the general pleasure of shaping a draft through small, reversible decisions. The included writing is fictional and contains no personal conversation or real user data.

## How to open
Open `index.html` in a modern browser, including from `file://`. No server, account, install, or network is needed. Drafts may be saved in this browser's local storage; they are not uploaded.

## Controls
Edit Draft A and Draft B; the differences recalculate automatically. Tap the A or B option inside any highlighted change, or use **All A**, **All B**, and **Reset choices**. Tap an option again to leave that change undecided; undecided changes follow A in the assembled draft. Swap drafts or load the example from the toolbar. Edit the final draft by hand; **Rebuild** discards those manual edits and resumes following the selections. **Copy final** and **Download .txt** export your text. With keyboard focus on a change, use arrow keys to move and A/B to choose.

## Why it was built
Comparing two drafts shouldn't require a server or a heavyweight document system. Revision Bench makes the little choices visible and turns them into usable text.

## Format choice
- **Lane:** `writing_or_decision_aid`
- **Mechanic:** word-level two-draft diff with tap-to-select alternate change hunks and an editable assembled final draft; copy/plain-text download.

## Explicit bans avoided
Not a field guide, atlas, almanac, codex, zine, branching adventure, signal/cartographer/constellation router, launch/readiness simulator, scorecard/rubric meter, abstract AI-agent preflight tool, or poetic generator.
