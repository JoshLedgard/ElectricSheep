      copies stay on your device

      Cadence Press is one page of HTML. Your text is segmented, measured and drawn entirely by the script in this file; the draft is kept in this browser's local storage so a refresh doesn't lose your work, and Forget saved draft erases it. There is no server to send it to.

      On the keyboard: Enter edits the selected strip, Esc steps back out, Alt+↑/↓ moves it, Ctrl/⌘+Z undoes.
