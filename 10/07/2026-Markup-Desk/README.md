# Markup Desk

## What it is
A private, one-file visual review bench. Open a local screenshot, place numbered pins or drag highlight boxes, write concise notes, and export a marked PNG plus a plain-text review handoff. A fictional sample screen and three sample notes make it immediately explorable.

## Privacy-safe inspiration
Inspired by the general craft of reviewing a visual artifact and turning feedback into an actionable handoff. The sample is invented; no personal, customer, project, or conversation data appears in it. Imported images and notes remain in your browser tab and are never uploaded.

## How to open
Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No server, account, install, or network connection is needed.

## Controls
- Tap **Open screenshot** to select an image from your device, or **Restore demo** for the sample.
- **Place pin:** tap the image. **Draw box:** drag across an area. Both work with mouse, touch, or pen.
- Type into the numbered note cards. Delete an individual mark, undo the most recent mark, or clear all marks.
- Hide marks temporarily to inspect the underlying image.
- **Download marked PNG** saves all numbered marks even if they are temporarily hidden; **Copy review text** copies the numbered notes, with a text-file fallback if clipboard access is unavailable.

## Why it was built
A useful micro-tool should let a reviewer go from “something feels off” to an annotated image and a crisp handoff in a couple of minutes, without a sign-in or new workflow.

## Format choice
- **Lane:** `practical_micro_tool`
- **Mechanic:** `touch-and-drag image markup with numbered editable notes and local exports`

## Explicit bans avoided
No field-guide/zine/atlas/almanac/codex framing, branching narrative, routing/constellation metaphor, launch/readiness simulator, scorecard/rubric meter, agent preflight, or generic generator. Unlike the preceding flicker game, sentence-strip editor, and geometric stamping game, this is a direct annotation workspace.
