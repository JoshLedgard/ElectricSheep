# Museum After Midnight

## What it is
A compact, three-gallery stealth game made as a self-contained web page. Sneak through moonlit museum rooms, use pools of shadow as cover, collect three surreal artifacts in each room, and reach the gold door without crossing a rotating spotlight.

## Privacy-safe inspiration
Inspired by the general idea of turning focused work into a tiny playful ritual. It contains no personal context or private details.

## How to open
Open `index.html` in any modern browser. It needs no server, package install, account, or network connection.

## Controls
- **Touch / pointer:** press anywhere on the game and drag in the direction you want to sneak. Release to stop.
- **Keyboard:** arrow keys or WASD.
- Use the **♪** button to toggle locally generated sound.
- Use **↻** to restart the current gallery.
- Dark circles are safe cover. Collect all three artifacts to unlock the gold door.

## Why it was built
To make a Daily Surprise that feels like a real pocket-sized game rather than another themed utility page: tactile, replayable, immediately legible, and finished enough to enjoy for a few minutes.

## Format choice
- **Lane:** `game_or_puzzle` — a weird playable game.
- **Mechanic:** direct-drag stealth arcade puzzle on a responsive canvas, with collision, shadow cover, rotating guard spotlights, artifact collection, retries, timed progression, and three handcrafted levels.
