# Queue Garden Field Guide

## What it is
A pocket, mobile-first **illustrated field guide** for noticing the small recurring shapes that live in any busy flow of work. You walk a short observation trail, meeting six hand-drawn "specimens" — the Waiting Fern, the Handoff Firefly, the Context Moss, the Tiny Next-Step Beetle, the Backlog Snail, and the Done Bell. Each specimen has a comic-style sketch panel, a **tap-to-reveal** practical observation, and one tap choice that reflects how your garden looks today. At the end, your choices fold into a short, copyable **field note** generated entirely on your device.

No scores, no meters, no checklists, no readiness language — just a calm, whimsical way to make a busy system feel a little more legible.

## Privacy-safe inspiration
Built from generic, non-private themes only: queues, handoffs, accumulating context, finding the next small step, and marking things done — i.e. making busy systems understandable without ever touching real work, names, or details. Nothing about any specific person, project, or conversation is referenced or stored.

## How to open
Double-click `index.html`, or open it in any modern browser (phone or desktop). No server, no internet, no install, no dependencies. Best viewed on a phone in portrait.

## Controls
- **Tap "Enter the garden"** to begin (or "How to read this guide" for a quick orientation).
- On each specimen, **tap the card** to reveal its habit / observation.
- **Tap a choice** to record how that part of your garden feels and advance.
- At the trail's end, **Copy field note** puts the generated note on your clipboard; **Walk the garden again** restarts.
- All touch targets are ≥44px; readable at 390px width.

## Why it was built
A Daily Surprise for Josh — a tiny thing Scoob might dream up to make the act of noticing workflow patterns feel gentle and concrete. It turns "lots of small moving items" into a handful of friendly creatures and one understandable next observation, ending with a keepsake you can paste anywhere.

## Format choice
- **Lane:** visual_story_or_comic
- **Mechanic:** mobile-first illustrated field-guide / comic with tap-to-reveal specimen cards and a tiny choose-your-own observation trail, ending in a locally generated copyable field note. No scoring, no readiness checks, no launch simulation.

## Avoided recent patterns
- Launch / readiness / shipping simulators
- Generic scorecard / rubric / meter visuals
- Abstract AI-agent context / preflight tools
- Card-sort / board mechanics
- Poetic / generic text generators

## Privacy & offline
Runs fully offline. Stores nothing — no localStorage, no cookies, no network calls. Closing the tab discards everything. The field note exists only in memory until you copy it.
