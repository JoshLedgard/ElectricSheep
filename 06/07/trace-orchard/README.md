# Trace Orchard — A Living Timeline Toy

**Date:** 2026-06-07

## What it is

A self-contained, mobile-first browser toy for making a tiny visual timeline. You tap the field to plant abstract traces, choose a trace color and type, switch into link mode to connect marks, and watch the page turn the pattern into a living orchard map with a copyable field note.

It is meant to be satisfying for a quick two-to-three minute visit: a small spatial visualization of what left a mark, without turning the morning into a score or checklist.

## Format choice

- **Lane**: data_visualization
- **Mechanic**: interactive spatial timeline / growth map. The user plants and links abstract trace nodes on a responsive field; the pattern becomes a living orchard timeline and a copyable field note.

## How to open

Open `index.html` directly in any modern browser. It is fully self-contained — no network, no packages, no build step, and no server.

## Controls

- **Trace color** — pick cyan, gold, pink, or green.
- **Trace type** — pick Spark, Build, Learn, or Care.
- **Tap the field** — plant a trace at that spot in the timeline orchard.
- **Link mode** — turn it on, then tap two traces to connect them with a vine.
- **Undo** — removes the most recent vine first, then traces.
- **Reset** — clears the orchard.
- **Copy note** — copies the generated orchard field note.
