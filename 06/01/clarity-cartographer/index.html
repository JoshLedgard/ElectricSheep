    How the score works

    Priority = (Reach × Pain × Confidence) ÷ Effort. Reach is how many visitors touch this surface. Pain is how much friction lives there. Confidence is how sure you are the fix will land. Effort is what shipping it costs. Higher score = ship sooner.

    Made for the curiosity of fuzzy support-site critique becoming concrete priorities. Self-contained, no network.
