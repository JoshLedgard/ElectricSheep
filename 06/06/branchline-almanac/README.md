# Branchline Almanac — A Tiny Choose-Your-Own Zine

**Date:** 2026-06-06

## What it is

A self-contained, mobile-first choose-your-own comic/zine. You tap through three tiny route choices — the workbench, the crossing, and the archive — while the illustrated panel changes. At the end, the route folds into a deterministic pocket almanac page with three stamps, a route name, and a copyable field note.

It is meant to feel like a two-to-three minute playful artifact: a little morning branchline that turns "where do I start?" into a fictional path, not a productivity audit.

## Format choice

- **Lane**: visual_story_or_comic
- **Mechanic**: tap-through branching comic/zine with route choices that alter the illustrated panel and produce a small copyable almanac page.

## Avoided recent patterns

This deliberately avoids the overused patterns from the last week:

- launch/readiness/shipping simulators;
- generic scorecard/rubric meters;
- abstract AI-agent context, preflight, prism, or debugger tools;
- card sorting, dial tuning, and allocation/constellation data toys.

## Privacy-safe inspiration

The safe theme was generic task/kanban energy plus the fallback mood of a fresh start, morning curiosity, and playful systems. No real task names, conversations, customers, family details, locations, finances, secrets, or private project specifics are used. The whole story is fictional.

## How to open

Open `index.html` directly in any modern browser. It runs entirely in the page: no network, no server, no packages, no fonts, no analytics, and no build step.

## Controls

- Tap one of three route choices in each chapter.
- Watch the comic panel, sign, and object update as the story moves.
- At the end, read the generated almanac page.
- Use **Copy almanac page** to copy the route note.
- Use **Reset route** to start again.

All tap targets are designed for mobile use and the layout is tuned for narrow screens.
