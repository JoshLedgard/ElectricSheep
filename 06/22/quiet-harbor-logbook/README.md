# Quiet Harbor Logbook

A mobile-first, calm creative ritual for starting (or closing) the day with one quiet minute at an imagined harbor.

## What it is
A tiny ambient logbook. You set the tide, tap lanterns alight on the water, take one slow guided breath, choose a focus for the day, and answer a single small prompt in one line. The app then composes a compact "harbor note" you can copy and keep — all generated locally on your device.

It is deliberately *not* a card sort, scorecard, rubric meter, readiness/launch simulator, preflight tool, or a generic poetry generator. The output is grounded in the concrete choices you tapped, so it feels like a log entry, not a slot-machine poem.

## Privacy-safe inspiration
Built from generic, non-private themes: recurring automations that quietly keep running, clean handoffs between days, and the feeling of beginning a day with a small steadying ritual. No private work details, names, or specifics are referenced anywhere.

## How to open
Open `index.html` in any modern browser (phone or desktop). No install, no server, no network — double-tap the file or open it from iCloud Drive. Everything runs offline and nothing leaves the device.

## Controls
- **1 · Set the tide** — tap one of Low / Rising / High / Ebbing. The sky scene retints accordingly.
- **2 · Lanterns & breath** — tap the floating lanterns on the water to light them; tap "Begin breathing" (or the glowing circle) for a slow in-hold-out-rest cycle.
- **3 · Choose a focus** — tap one chip: Tend, Carry, Release, Notice, or Steady.
- **4 · One line** — answer the prompt in a single sentence (↻ shuffles to another prompt).
- **Compose harbor note** — reveals your note; **Copy note** puts it on the clipboard, **New entry** clears the line for another pass.

## Why it was built
As today's Daily Surprise: a small, self-contained artifact that's understandable in under 10 seconds and pleasant for two or three minutes. The brief asked for a calm ritual that feels concrete (real taps, real choices) rather than abstract, and that can be verified at a glance without exposing anything private.

- **Mechanic:** mobile-first ambient logbook — a drag-free tap-and-compose ritual with tide phases, tappable tide lanterns, a breathing timer, tiny prompt cards, and a locally generated, copyable "harbor note."
