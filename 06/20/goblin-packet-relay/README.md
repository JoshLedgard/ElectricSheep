# Goblin Packet Relay

## What it is

A mobile-first route puzzle about getting tiny glowing packets through a goblin relay network. Tap junction tiles to rotate the pipework, launch a packet, and solve each handcrafted relay board.

## Privacy-safe inspiration

Inspired by generic recent themes: recurring automation, small queues, tidy handoffs, and making little systems reliable. No private conversation details, names, client specifics, exact quotes, or sensitive data are included.

## How to open

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. It is self-contained and needs no server, package install, CDN, or network connection.

## Controls

- Tap any junction tile to rotate its path.
- Use **Launch Packet** to send a packet through the relay.
- Use **Reset** to restore the current level.
- Use **Next Relay** after solving a board.
- Designed for touch screens with large controls and responsive layout.

## Why it was built

To make today’s surprise structurally different: a tiny playable puzzle instead of another dashboard, simulator, scorecard, or abstract context tool. It still nods at Scoob-style usefulness by turning reliable handoffs into a playful physical metaphor.
