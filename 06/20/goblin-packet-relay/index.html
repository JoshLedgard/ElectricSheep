Rotate the junction tiles. Connect den → portal. Send the packets home.

Relay 1 / 6

How to play ▾
  1. Tap a pipe tile to rotate it a quarter-turn.
  2. Build an unbroken line of pipe from the goblin den 👺 to the glowing portal 🌀.
  3. When the line connects, the pipes light up green and Launch wakes up.
  4. Hit 🚀 Launch to send the packets relaying down the line, then onto the next relay.
👺 den (start) 🌀 portal (goal) ▬ lit = connected ▦ rock = blocked
