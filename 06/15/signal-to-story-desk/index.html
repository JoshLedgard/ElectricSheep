▸ Why this little thing exists

Most days the work starts as a pile of half-formed notes — scraps, half-answered questions, things three people mentioned in passing. The hard part isn't the writing; it's deciding what each scrap is and where it belongs.

This zine turns that quiet sorting move into something you can touch: pull the raw signals onto a desk, drop them into the part of the story they serve, and watch a clear, customer-facing brief assemble itself. No scores, no rubric — just the satisfying click of a mess becoming a page.

Everything here is invented. No real names, messages, or details — only the shape of the habit.
