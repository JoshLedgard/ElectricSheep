# Momentum Loom — Daily Surprise, 2026-06-16

## What it is
A warm, textile-themed mini visualization. You allocate a fictional pool of
morning energy across three colored threads — **Anchor** (one deep, unhurried
thing), **Tend** (gathering loose ends and handoffs), and **Roam** (curiosity, a
fresh question) — using big touch sliders. A braided ribbon weaves and gently
drifts to reflect your balance, and a tap toggle chooses which thread "leads" the
braid. From the chosen balance it generates a tiny, copyable three-line
**Momentum Card** to set an intention for the day. No scores, no grades, no
pass/fail — just a calm little ritual.

## Privacy-safe inspiration
Fresh start, morning curiosity, and playful systems — plus the generic feeling of
weaving scattered operational threads into one calm, deliberate handoff. All copy
is fictional and generic; nothing references real people, places, or work.

## How to open
Open `index.html` in any modern browser (desktop or phone). It is a single
self-contained file — no install, no server, no network connection required.
Best viewed on a phone in portrait.

## Controls
- **Three sliders** (Anchor / Tend / Roam): set how much morning energy each
  thread carries. Percentages update live and re-weave the ribbon.
- **Lead chips**: tap to choose which thread sits on top of the braid and frames
  the card's intention.
- **Reset**: return to the starting balance.
- **Shuffle weave**: roll a fresh random balance and lead thread.
- **Copy card**: copies the three-line Momentum Card to your clipboard.

## Why it was built
A daily surprise meant to feel like a calm morning ritual: take a handful of
scattered intentions, weave them into one balanced plan, and walk away with a
single small first move. The loom metaphor turns abstract "how should I spend my
energy" into something tactile and pleasant to fiddle with for a couple of minutes.

## Format choice
- **Lane:** data_visualization
- **Mechanic:** an interactive woven timeline/flow diagram — touch sliders and
  tap toggles allocate fictional morning energy across three colored threads; the
  artifact animates a braided day-plan ribbon (responsive SVG, gentle drift,
  reduced-motion aware) and produces a tiny non-scored ritual card.
