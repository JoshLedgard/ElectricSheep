# Typewave Studio

## What it is
A self-contained kinetic-typography instrument. Draw a freehand curve, place a phrase on it, tune its drift, wave, and type size, swap palettes, and export the canvas as a PNG.

## Privacy-safe inspiration
Inspired generically by the pleasure of inspecting and directly shaping how a tool behaves. It contains no details from private conversations or personal data.

## How to open
Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No server, account, package install, or network connection is required.

## Controls
- Drag or touch the canvas to draw a new curve.
- Enter a phrase and press **Set phrase** (or Enter).
- Tune **Drift**, **Wave**, and **Type size**.
- Pause/play motion, cycle three palettes, restore the demo curve, or export a PNG.

## Why it was built
To make a tiny creative instrument that rewards direct manipulation and produces something worth saving in two or three minutes.

## Format choice
