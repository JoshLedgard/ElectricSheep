# Weekprint

## What it is

Weekprint is a tactile attention-planning prototype disguised as a tiny print shop. Choose one of five “inks”—Build, Think, People, Admin, or Wild—then paint across 28 broad moments in a week. A live proportional ribbon and two plain-language observations reveal the composition without grading it. The result can be copied as a compact attention receipt.

## Privacy-safe inspiration

The broad inspiration was coordinated workflows and the value of seeing a system as a whole. That theme became an abstract, personal attention map. No names, private messages, schedules, projects, or other personal details appear in the artifact.

## How to open

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. It is self-contained: no server, install, account, network request, external asset, or package is required.

## Controls

- Choose an ink: **Build**, **Think**, **People**, **Admin**, or **Wild**.
- Tap or drag across the 28 blocks to paint broad intentions.
- Paint a block with its current ink again to erase it.
- **Undo** lifts the latest impression.
- **Clear** starts a fresh sheet.
- **Copy receipt** copies a text summary when clipboard access is available.
- Press **?** for the compact in-app explanation.

## Why it was built

Calendar views make time look equally available and to-do lists hide proportion. Weekprint offers a different lens: before naming individual tasks, see the texture and balance of the attention you intend to spend. It is deliberately descriptive rather than prescriptive—there is no ideal distribution and no score.

## Format choice

- **Lane:** `writing_or_decision_aid`, delivered as a future-artifact / fake-app product prototype. Its live visual is feedback for an attention-allocation decision rather than a data-visualization showcase.
- **Mechanic:** tactile grid painting with five semantic inks, feeding a live proportional ribbon and a copyable text receipt.

## Explicit bans avoided

Weekprint is **not** a field guide, zine, atlas, almanac, codex, choose-your-own or branching story, microquest, signal-routing/cartographer/constellation/path-connection puzzle, launch/readiness/shipping simulator, scorecard, rubric meter, AI context/preflight/debugger, generic generator, or musical step sequencer.

## Privacy and implementation

The page contains no real names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, financial information, conversation quotes, trackers, analytics, storage, or network calls. All HTML, CSS, and JavaScript live inside `index.html`; input remains in memory only and disappears when the page closes. Buttons meet a 44px minimum target at a 390px viewport, keyboard interaction is supported, and reduced-motion preferences are respected.
