# Conviction Market

## What it is

A tactile, local-first decision instrument. Name four ideas, then distribute a fixed bank of twenty conviction chips among them across three time horizons: **Now**, **Next**, and **Later**. The constraint makes tradeoffs visible without pretending there is a mathematically correct winner.

## Privacy-safe inspiration

Inspired by a generic theme from recent context: making dense work systems easier to reason about, and turning fuzzy judgment into a concrete interaction. It contains no private context, personal details, or real project information.

## How to open

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. It is a self-contained file: no server, package install, login, external assets, analytics, or network connection is required.

## Controls

- Tap an idea name to replace it with your own.
- Use **+** to move one chip to an idea; the app automatically takes that chip from the strongest alternative.
- Use **−** to move one chip away; it goes to the least-funded alternative.
- Switch among **Now**, **Next**, and **Later** to maintain and compare three distinct allocations.
- Use **Copy snapshot** to copy all three horizons as plain text.
- Use **Reset** to restore the invented sample market.

All state is stored only in browser `localStorage`.

## Why it was built

Priority lists hide tradeoffs: everything can look important at once. Conviction Market turns prioritization into a small physical-feeling act. It is deliberately not a scoring system. You are recording how attention moves over time, not grading ideas or asking an algorithm to pick a winner.

## Privacy and verification notes

The included idea names are fictional and generic. The page contains no real names, contact details, addresses, financial data, conversation quotes, employer/client references, analytics, or network requests. The chip total is fixed at twenty for every horizon, and the allocation controls preserve that invariant by moving—not creating or deleting—chips.
