# Bitbeat Foundry

## What it is

A tiny tactile music machine: paint notes onto an 8×8 grid, press Play, and hear the pattern sweep from left to right. Turn on **Evolve** and a cellular-automaton rule reshapes the composition after each loop, making the rhythm audibly and visibly wander.

## Inspiration

A privacy-safe, generic theme of playful systems and repeatable workflows—translated into something you can touch, hear, interrupt, and reshape.

## How to open

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. It is a self-contained file: no server, account, package install, network connection, or remote assets are required. Audio begins only after pressing **Play**, as required by mobile browsers.

## Controls

- **Tap or drag** across the grid to paint or erase notes.
- **Play / Stop** starts or pauses the eight-beat loop.
- **Evolve** toggles cellular-automaton mutation after each completed loop.
- **Tempo** changes playback from 70 to 170 BPM.
- **New pattern** creates a curated rhythmic starting point with a fresh melodic texture.
- **Clear** empties the grid.

The current grid, tempo, and Evolve setting are saved locally in the browser. Nothing is transmitted.

## Why it was built

The goal was a compact artifact that rewards both deliberate editing and surprise: a useful little sketchpad for rhythm, but also a toy that can escape the exact loop you gave it. It is designed for a satisfying two- or three-minute session on a phone or desktop.
