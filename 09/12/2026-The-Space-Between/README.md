# The Space Between

## What it is

A tactile optical-kerning game disguised as an old print-shop proof room. Eight troublesome letter pairs appear at poster scale. Drag the glyphs until the negative space feels balanced, pull a proof, and compare your eye with a hidden optical target. At the end, the game draws a visual signature of your choices.

## Privacy-safe inspiration

Built from the broad theme of human judgment: some qualities are easy to measure but still need an eye, an ear, or a feel for the work. The experience contains no personal context or real-world data.

## How to open

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. It is a single self-contained file: no server, account, network connection, packages, or external assets required.

## Controls

- Drag horizontally across the large type specimen to tighten or loosen the pair.
- Use the slider or **− / +** buttons for precise touch control.
- On a keyboard, use the left/right arrow keys and press Enter to pull a proof.
- Complete eight pairs to reveal your spacing signature.

## Why it was built

The page turns a nearly invisible design craft into a physical-feeling two-minute puzzle. It is deliberately unrelated to agent consoles, interface inspections, release simulations, and abstract system metaphors—a fresh creative-coding lane after recent product and data experiments.
