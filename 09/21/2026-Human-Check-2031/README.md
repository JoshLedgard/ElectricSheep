# HUMAN//CHECK 2031

## What it is

A polished fake civic-identity app from the near future: synthetic behavior has become flawless, so people verify themselves through four tiny acts of imperfection. The experience is a fast, linear reverse-CAPTCHA gauntlet built for touch, mouse, and keyboard-friendly controls.

## Privacy-safe inspiration

Recent creative energy centered on making complex automated systems legible and trustworthy. HUMAN//CHECK turns that broad theme sideways into a playful fictional interface about the value of human timing, asymmetry, wandering gestures, and subjective judgment. It uses no personal details or private source material.

## How to open

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. It is a single self-contained file: no server, package install, account, external assets, or network connection is required.

## Controls

1. **Instinct:** tap the center button when the violet pulse nearly reaches the dashed ring.
2. **Perception:** tap the subtly imperfect mark in each grid.
3. **Gesture:** draw one long, wandering stroke in the canvas using a finger, pointer, or mouse.
4. **Intuition:** press and hold the circular control, then release when a “moment” feels complete.
5. Use **Replay Test** on the result screen to run the full test again.

All primary touch targets are at least 44px. The layout is designed for a 390px-wide phone, scales to desktop, avoids hover-only behavior, and respects reduced-motion preferences.

## Why it was built

This is a small “artifact from the future” that asks a strange product question: if machines become better than us at acting polished, could imperfection become a credential? The goal is two to three minutes of tactile play with the visual finish and internal logic of a real product prototype.

## Format choice

- **Lane:** `game_or_puzzle` — presented as an artifact-from-the-future / fake app.
- **Mechanic:** a linear, replayable multi-round reverse-CAPTCHA gauntlet using timing, visual anomaly spotting, freehand gesture, and press-and-hold intuition.
