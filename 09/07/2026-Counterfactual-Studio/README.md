# Counterfactual Studio

## What it is

A self-contained, near-future product-thinking instrument. It holds one fictional future steady while you alter another, then visualizes how adoption, trust, and support work change over 90 days. It is a sandbox for noticing second-order effects—not a forecast, grade, or recommendation.

## Privacy-safe inspiration

Inspired by the general challenge of making complex product systems easier to reason about. Every scenario, assumption, and data point is fictional and generic; no private conversation or personal detail is represented.

## How to open

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. It needs no server, login, network connection, external asset, or package installation.

## Controls

- Choose one of three fictional product decisions.
- Adjust the four **Future B assumptions** while **Future A** remains fixed.
- Scrub the 90-day timeline to inspect a particular moment.
- **Play timeline** animates the comparison.
- **Apply a shock** cycles through deterministic assumption changes.
- **Make B the baseline** turns the altered future into a new reference.
- **Reset B** restores the current scenario's initial assumptions.

The solid curves represent adoption, the thinner curves trust, and the dashed curves support load. Teal is Future A; amber is Future B.

## Why it was built

Many product choices look simple in a static table. This prototype makes delayed tradeoffs tangible: a small change can help one outcome early while creating a different burden later. It is meant to be played with, not obeyed.

## Format choice

- **Lane:** `data_visualization / future-artifact product prototype`
- **Mechanic:** manipulate tangible assumption controls, compare two parallel futures in animated curves, and scrub a 90-day timeline to inspect second-order effects.

## Explicit bans avoided

No field guide, atlas, almanac, codex, bestiary, zine, or pocket-guide framing; no choose-your-own or branching story, microquest, or multiple endings; no signal routing, cartography, constellation, paths, or connect-the-nodes metaphor; no launch/readiness/shipping simulator; no scorecard or rubric meter; no AI-agent context/preflight/debugger; no poetic generator; and no family, travel, baseball, home, or location-specific utility.

## Privacy check

The artifact contains no real names, email addresses, phone numbers, street addresses, credentials, private quotes, financial details, or identifiable personal/project data. All product examples and model values are fictional.
