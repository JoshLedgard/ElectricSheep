# Crowd in a Bottle — Landing Page Wind Tunnel

## What it is
A polished, tactile fake product-research app. Edit a tiny landing page, choose one of three fictional crowd temperaments, and release 24 animated visitors into the chamber. They skim, pause, and tap according to a small set of transparent, deterministic rules. Change one thing and replay to see the behavioral mix shift.

This is a playful thought instrument, **not analytics, prediction, user research, or scientific evidence**. All visitors and outcomes are synthetic and illustrative.

## Privacy-safe inspiration
Built from a generic interest in modern product-interface design and in making product decisions tangible. It contains no real people, organizations, products, projects, accounts, conversations, or datasets.

Any headline or button text you enter stays only in the open browser tab. It is not persisted or transmitted.

## How to open
Open `index.html` in any modern browser. It is one self-contained file: no server, install, package, network connection, external font, or external asset is required.

## Controls
- Pick **Curious**, **Hurried**, or **Skeptical** to change the fictional behavior rules.
- Edit the sample headline and button text.
- Adjust supporting detail and proof marks.
- Tap **Release 24 fictional visitors** to run the chamber.
- Tap **Replay** to repeat deterministically, or **Reset** to restore the original setup.

## Why it was built
Landing-page discussions can become abstract quickly. This makes the conversation physical and inspectable: a tiny page, a crowd you can see, and a deliberately simplified rule ledger. The useful move is not believing the outcome; it is changing one variable and noticing which assumptions the toy makes visible.

## Format choice
- **Lane:** `startup_growth_experiment`
- **Mechanic:** A tactile, real-feeling fake-app sandbox with an editable landing-page mockup and animated deterministic visitor waves. It shows a distribution of illustrative behaviors and plain-language observations rather than a grade or optimization score.

This lane and mechanic are structurally different from the recent data instrument, card-sorting micro-tool, and visual-story formats.

- No choose-your-own, branching story, microquest, or multiple-ending mechanic.
- No signal, cartographer, constellation, routing, or path-connection metaphor.
- No launch, readiness, or shipping simulator.
- No scorecard, rubric meter, grading, or single optimization score.
- No preflight, context debugger, or abstract AI-agent context tool.
- No poetic generator or visual comic.

## Privacy and accessibility checks
- All included names, counts, visitors, outcomes, and behavior rules are fictional.
- No personal names, contact details, addresses, financial data, private quotes, or sensitive specifics appear.
- Responsive at phone widths, including 390px; controls have touch-friendly targets.
- Keyboard focus is visible, contrast is high, and reduced-motion preferences are respected.
