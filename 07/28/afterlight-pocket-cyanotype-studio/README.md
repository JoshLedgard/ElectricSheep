# Afterlight — A Pocket Cyanotype Studio

*Daily Surprise · 2026-07-28*

## What it is

Afterlight is a self-contained creative instrument that turns a phone or browser into a fictional cyanotype workbench. Arrange original botanical and geometric silhouettes on a sensitized sheet, move and reshape them directly, hold the exposure control to develop the coating, then swipe across the paper to rinse it into deep Prussian blue. The finished composition exports as a real high-resolution PNG.

A complete demo arrangement is loaded on opening, so the compose → expose → rinse → export loop is understandable immediately. Afterlight is an artistic simulation, not actual chemistry: it uses no camera or light sensor and makes no scientific claims.

Everything runs locally in the open tab. There are no accounts, uploads, analytics, network requests, external assets, saved browser data, packages, or server.

## Privacy-safe inspiration

The broad inspiration was the generic pleasure of calmly shaping busy material into something tangible, combined with fresh-start, morning-curiosity, and playful-systems energy. Afterlight contains no real people, organizations, projects, conversations, accounts, or datasets.

## How to open

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No install, build step, server, account, or internet connection is required.

## Controls

- Tap a silhouette in the horizontal palette to add it to the sheet.
- Drag a selected silhouette to move it.
- Drag its corner handle—or use two fingers—to rotate and scale it.
- Use the large controls to turn, resize, mirror, duplicate, bring forward, or remove the selection.
- Use **Undo**, **Demo**, or **Bare sheet** to reshape the composition.
- Press and hold **Hold to expose**. A short hold creates a paler result; about three seconds creates a deep print.
- Swipe left to right across the sheet to rinse it, or use **Rinse it for me**.
- Add a short caption and choose **Export PNG** to download the finished print.
- **Recompose**, **Re-expose**, and **New sheet** support another pass without reloading.

All primary touch targets are at least 44px, pointer controls also work with a mouse, exposure works from the keyboard, and reduced-motion preferences are respected.
