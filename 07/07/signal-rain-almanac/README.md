# Signal Rain Almanac

## Title
Signal Rain Almanac — Daily Surprise for 2026-07-07

## What it is
A tactile, ambient day-timeline. Colored "signal drops" drift down a small
morning sky split into three time bands — **Morning · Midday · Evening**. Tap a
drop to gather it into the hour it's falling through; each catch sends out a
soft ripple, adds a colored mark to that band's shore, and rewrites a tiny
plain-language almanac note describing the shape of your day. No scores, no
grades, no meters, no readiness or launch language — just watching a scatter of
signals slowly become legible.

Five drop colors stand in for generic, made-up signal kinds: **signal, trail,
evidence, handoff, curiosity**. They mean nothing private — they're just
weather.

## Privacy-safe inspiration
Built from generic, non-private themes of *watching evidence trails and
handoffs become legible over time*, and of *morning curiosity* — the everyday
feeling of a scatter of little signals gradually settling into a readable
picture. There are **no real names, accounts, business details, quotes, dollar
amounts, emails, addresses, or sensitive specifics** anywhere in it. Every drop
and every almanac line is synthesized from a fixed word list on the device.

## How to open
Open `index.html` in any modern mobile or desktop browser (double-tap the file,
or drag it into a browser tab). No install, no network, no accounts, no
external assets — it runs fully offline in a single file. Best on a phone.

## Controls
- **Tap a drifting drop** — gathers it into the band it is over; plays a ripple.
- **Gather Morning / Midday / Evening** buttons — catch the lowest drop in that
  band (or drop a fresh one there). Keyboard-friendly, 44px targets.
- **Number keys 1 / 2 / 3** — same as the three Gather buttons.
- **🌧️ Gentle new rain** — drifts a fresh handful of drops into the sky.
- **📋 Copy note** — copies the current almanac note to your clipboard.
- **↺ Reset** — clears the sky and the shore back to a still morning.
- A light baseline rain keeps drifting on its own so there's always something to
  tap. Honors `prefers-reduced-motion` (drops hold still; tapping still works).

## Why it was built
The recent surprises leaned on card-sorts, route/constellation puzzles, and
visual-story zines, and the cooldown list rules out launch/readiness/shipping
simulators, scorecard/rubric meters, and abstract AI-agent preflight tools. This
picks a different register entirely: a calm, non-judgmental **data
visualization** you can play in a couple of minutes — the pleasure is in the
accumulation and the small legible summary, not in winning or being scored.

## Format choice
- **Lane:** data_visualization
- **Mechanic:** tactile ambient timeline / rainfall-of-signals. Drops drift down
  a three-band day timeline; tapping accumulates them into colored ripples and a
  local-only observation note. Purely observational — no scoring, grading,
  readiness state, or launch framing.

## Avoided recent patterns
- Not a launch / readiness / shipping simulator.
- Not a scorecard / rubric / meter.
- Not an abstract AI-agent context / preflight tool.
- Not another card-sort "loom" (Jul 6 Thread Loom).
- Not a constellation route puzzle (Jul 4 / Jul 3 cartographers).
- Not a visual-story comic / zine (Jul 5 Field Guide).

## Storage
Your gathered tallies and note are saved **only** in this browser via
`localStorage` (key `signal-rain-almanac-2026-07-07`). Nothing is uploaded or
shared; Reset clears it.
