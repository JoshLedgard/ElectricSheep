# Soft Dig — A Pocket Excavation Puzzle

*Daily Surprise · 2026-07-30*

## What it is

Soft Dig is a self-contained tactile browser game about uncovering three harmless fictional objects from compact sediment trays. Brush a steady path, puff away a wide surface layer, and use a wooden pick around tight edges. Once enough of a buried silhouette is uncovered, lift it gently and open the next tray.

The loopstone, moon key, and rain cup are invented objects with no historical or scientific claims attached. Nothing is scored, graded, timed, or breakable. A playable tray is ready as soon as the page opens, and all three discoveries take roughly two to three minutes.

Everything runs locally in the open browser tab. There are no accounts, uploads, analytics, network requests, external assets, packages, saved browser data, or server.

## How to open

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No installation, server, account, or network connection is required. It is designed for touch at 390px and wider, and also supports a mouse or pen.

## Controls

- **Brush:** drag to clear a steady, medium-width path.
- **Air puff:** drag for a broad, gentle surface pass.
- **Wood pick:** drag to clean tight corners along a visible edge.
- **Edges uncovered:** descriptive progress toward making the object liftable; it is not a score or grade.
- **Lift gently:** becomes available when the silhouette is sufficiently exposed.
- **Refill tray:** restarts the current object.
- **Bury them again:** restarts all three trays after the final discovery.

Sound is synthesized quietly and locally after interaction. Motion honors the system's reduced-motion preference. No relic can be damaged; this is playful fiction, not archaeological instruction.
