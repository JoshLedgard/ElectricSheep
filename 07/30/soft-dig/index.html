How does this little tray work?

Brush clears a steady path. Air puff moves a broad, thin layer. Wood pick works in tight corners. Tool speed changes the marks, but nothing is scored and no relic can be damaged.

All sand, shapes, and sound are made locally in this tab. Soft Dig is playful fiction, not archaeological instruction.
