# Desk Arcade: Bug Buffet

## What it is
A tiny, playable desk-cleanup toy. Little cartoon "bugs" clutter a messy desk, and you
flick each one into one of three trays — **Fix**, **Later**, or **Nope**. As the desk
clears, a progress bar fills and the workspace transforms into a clean, copyable
triage note (Fix / Later / Nope). It's a weird little utility that turns messy,
buzzing triage clutter into a small satisfying cleanup ritual — not a scorecard.

Everything is synthetic and local: 12 whimsical invented bug cards, plus an optional
"add your own bug" box that stays on your device.

## Privacy-safe inspiration
The generic feeling of turning a pile of messy verification/QA clutter into a small,
satisfying, physical-feeling cleanup — the moment where a chaotic desk of "did you
check this?" notes finally gets sorted into a short, honest list. No real people,
projects, tools, or chats — just the vibe of tidying triage.

## How to open
Open `index.html` in any modern browser (desktop or mobile). No install, no server,
no network — it's a single self-contained file. On a phone, just tap to open it.

## Controls
- **Tap a card's tray button** to cycle it: Unsorted → 🔧 Fix → 🕒 Later → 🚫 Nope → back to Unsorted.
- **Keyboard:** focus a card's button (Tab), then press `1` = Fix, `2` = Later, `3` = Nope, `0`/Backspace = Unsorted.
- **🔀 New batch** — scramble a fresh set of 8–12 bugs.
- **✨ Send rest to Later** — auto-file everything still unsorted.
- **↺ Reset** — put all current bugs back on the desk.
- **➕ Add your own bug** — type a custom item (stored only in this page, never sent anywhere).
- **📄 Copy note** / **⬇︎ Download** — grab the triage note as text.

Sort every card and the desk shows a "Desk cleared" state; the triage note updates live below.

## Why it was built
A daily surprise: a mobile-first, self-contained micro-tool that feels like a weird
little arcade utility. The goal was something immediately understandable, satisfying
for 2–3 minutes, and genuinely a bit useful — you walk away with a concise
Fix / Later / Nope note you can paste anywhere.

## Privacy
No real names, organizations, projects, emails, addresses, money, credentials, or
private chat quotes. All bug cards are invented and generic. Custom text stays in the
page and is never transmitted.
