# Ghost Frame — A Pocket Rotoscope

## What it is
Ghost Frame is a small, self-contained frame-by-frame animation instrument. Draw directly on four frames with a finger, pen, or mouse; use the pink and blue onion-skin ghosts to line up neighboring poses; then play the loop and export the four drawings as a real PNG sprite sheet.

A built-in bouncing-ball guide is ready as soon as the page opens, so the entire trace → play → export loop can be tried in two or three minutes. You can also import a local image onto any frame as a tracing reference. This is a creative instrument, not a generator: every visible animation stroke comes from you.

Everything runs locally in the open browser tab. There are no accounts, uploads, analytics, network requests, external assets, or saved browser data.

## Privacy-safe inspiration
The broad inspiration was an interest in making asynchronous processes tangible and creating small visual artifacts that are easy to share. That generic theme became four hand-drawn moments assembled into one looping object. Ghost Frame contains no real people, organizations, projects, messages, conversations, accounts, or datasets.

Imported images are decoded locally with the browser's `FileReader` and remain in the current tab. They are not persisted or transmitted.

## How to open
Open `index.html` directly in any modern browser. No server, installation, package, login, or internet connection is required.

## Controls
- **Frame 1–4:** select a drawing frame.
- **Draw on the paper:** add strokes with touch, pen, or mouse.
- **Play/Pause:** preview the four-frame loop.
- **Speed:** choose 2–12 frames per second.
- **Onion skin / Ghost:** toggle and adjust neighboring-frame overlays.
- **Color / Size:** choose the brush color and width.
- **Undo / Clear frame:** revise the current frame.
- **Demo guide:** restore the four-pose bouncing-ball tracing guide without deleting your strokes.
- **Import:** load a local image as the current frame's reference.
- **Guide:** show or hide the reference underlay.
- **Export PNG:** download a 1280 × 320 sprite sheet containing four 320 × 320 cells.

## Why it was built
The goal was a surprising but immediately legible creative-coding tool: something tactile enough to feel like a tiny animation desk, useful without permissions, and capable of producing a genuine artifact rather than a score, recommendation, or generated paragraph.

## Format choice
- **Lane:** `calm_creative_ritual` — presented as a polished creative-coding instrument and believable pocket animation app.
- **Mechanic:** direct-manipulation four-frame rotoscoping with touch drawing, adjustable onion-skin overlays, loop playback, local reference-image import, and sprite-sheet export.

This lane and interaction model differ from the previous three surprises' practical scratchpad, gesture observatory, and manuscript editing mechanics.
