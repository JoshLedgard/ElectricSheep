# Breakpoint — A Headline Lockup Press

## What it is

Breakpoint is a self-contained responsive typography press. Type one headline and an optional kicker, then work directly with the joints between words: keep phrases together, force intentional breaks, and watch square, portrait, and landscape lockups reset themselves at once. Three tactile visual treatments, safe-area overlays, plain-language fit notices, and real SVG exports turn the experiment into a usable production artifact.

A harmless fictional example is loaded on opening, so the complete edit → compare → export loop is ready immediately.

## Privacy-safe inspiration

The broad, generic inspiration was an interest in turning dense ideas into concise visual labels and helping content hierarchy survive across several formats. Breakpoint contains no real people, organizations, projects, conversations, accounts, or datasets.

Anything typed into the press stays only in the open browser tab. The page has no network requests, analytics, uploads, external assets, or persistent storage.

## How to open

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No server, install, account, or network connection is required.

## Controls

- Edit the **kicker** and **headline** fields.
- Tap the joints between headline words to change how neighboring words behave: free, bonded together, or intentionally broken onto separate lines.
- Switch among the three visual treatments.
- Toggle safe-area guides to inspect the printable/visible region.
- Compare the synchronized **square**, **portrait**, and **landscape** lockups.
- Use the export controls to download production-ready SVG artwork, including a contact sheet.
- Reset to restore the built-in example.

Fit messages are descriptive production notices, not grades, ratings, predictions, or optimization scores.

## Why it was built

Short labels often have to keep their character while moving among cards, posts, banners, and slides. Breakpoint makes that normally fiddly responsive-typography problem tangible: one phrase, three shapes, and explicit control over which words should travel together. It is meant to feel like a compact tool from a real creative workflow rather than a themed webpage.

## Format choice

- **Lane:** `startup_growth_experiment` — a polished product prototype for shaping campaign or product language across real publishing formats, without pretending to predict performance.
- **Mechanic:** direct-manipulation responsive typography press. The user edits one headline, taps word joints to bond phrases or force line breaks, compares three synchronized aspect-ratio lockups, toggles treatments and safe-area guides, receives neutral overflow notices, and exports actual SVG artifacts.

This lane differs from the last three successful surprises' practical screenshot workbench, agent-memory prototype, and creative rotoscope. Its word-joint typesetting mechanic differs from cropping/redaction, darkroom timeline controls, and freehand frame drawing.
