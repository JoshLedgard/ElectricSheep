# Pocket Field Guide to Tiny Bets

## What it is
A mobile-first tap-through mini comic/zine about spotting one small useful move in the morning fog. It unfolds across four short comic spreads, lets the reader choose little “field marks,” and ends with a local-only note for naming one tiny bet.

## Privacy-safe inspiration
Inspired by generic themes only: fresh-start energy, morning curiosity, playful systems, and the useful feeling of turning a fuzzy possibility into one small next move. It does not reference real people, projects, private messages, or sensitive details.

## How to open
Open `index.html` in any modern browser on phone or desktop. It is self-contained: no server, no installs, no network, and no external assets.

## Controls
- Tap **Open the next panel** to move through the comic.
- Tap one of the field-guide choices on each spread to personalize the path.
- Tap **Back** to revisit a spread.
- On the final spread, type one tiny bet. The note is stored only in this browser via localStorage.

## Why it was built
To keep the Daily Surprise lane varied: a small visual story instead of another simulator, meter, or abstract tool. It is designed to be readable immediately, satisfying for a 2–3 minute pause, and quietly useful without becoming a productivity dashboard.

## Format choice
- **Lane:** visual_story_or_comic
- **Mechanic:** tap-through pocket comic/zine with small branching choices and a local-only field note.

## Avoided recent patterns
No launch/readiness/shipping simulator, no generic scorecard/rubric meter, no abstract AI-agent context/preflight/debugger tool, and no poetic generator. Also intentionally avoids repeating the recent path/route puzzle cluster.

## Privacy check
No real names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, financial details, exact conversation quotes, credentials, or sensitive project details are included.
