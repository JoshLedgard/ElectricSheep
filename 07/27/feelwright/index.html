What this is. A local design instrument for sketching how a control should feel when it responds. It is not measurement, analytics, prediction, or optimization, and it does not represent any real product.

Honesty notes. Pulse strength comes from how long you hold a tap, not from finger pressure — browsers don't expose that reliably. Vibration is offered only where the browser reports support, and phones may ignore it. Sound starts only after you tap, because audio requires a gesture.

Privacy. Everything runs in this tab. No network requests, no storage, no uploads, no analytics. Close the tab and the pattern is gone — export first if you want to keep it.
