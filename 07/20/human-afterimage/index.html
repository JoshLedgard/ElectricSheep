How to read it. The observatory records only the stroke you draw, on your device, with nothing sent anywhere. Speed is distance over time between samples. Curvature is how sharply the direction turns at each step. Gesture energy is derived from speed as an honest proxy for how firmly you seemed to press — pointers report no real pressure here, so nothing is measured. Stillness rises where your hand hesitated or paused. Scrub the timeline to move the marker through every metric at once.
