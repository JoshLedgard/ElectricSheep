The bench

Draftlight is a hands-on editing instrument, not a grader. Paste any prose and it becomes movable strips — one per sentence — lit from beneath. It describes what it sees (long stretches, repeated openings, nearby echoes) and lets you focus, dim, reorder, and edit. No scores. No advice. Just clearer light on your own words.
