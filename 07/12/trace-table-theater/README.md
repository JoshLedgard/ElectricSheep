# Trace Table Theater

## What it is
A self-contained, mobile-first choose-your-own mini comic. Tap through six tiny theater scenes, collect observation cards, and copy a short debrief at the end.

## Privacy-safe inspiration
Inspired by the general shape of hands-on product review work: following a user journey, collecting visual evidence, naming friction clearly, and turning messy notes into a useful next step. It does not include private project details, names, emails, credentials, quotes, or sensitive context.

## How to open
Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No server, packages, network access, or external assets are required.

## Controls
- Tap an option card to choose what the scout notices next.
- Use **Next scene**, **Back**, and **Restart** to move through the story.
- Tap **Copy tiny debrief** to copy the collected observations.

## Why it was built
To make today’s surprise structurally different from recent spatial/path puzzles and from the banned launch/readiness/scorecard/preflight patterns: a short visual story that still feels like a useful little dreamed-up Scoob artifact for clearer product thinking.

## Format choice
- lane: visual_story_or_comic
- mechanic: choose-your-own explainer / tap-through mini comic with collectible observation cards

## Avoided recent patterns
- launch/readiness/shipping simulators
- generic scorecard/rubric meters
- abstract AI-agent context/preflight tools
- repeated path/routing puzzle feel from recent ElectricSheep entries

## Privacy check
Passed: no real names, emails, addresses, financial data, credentials, exact quotes, private project names, or sensitive conversation details are included.
