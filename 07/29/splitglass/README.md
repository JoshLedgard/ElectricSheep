# Splitglass — A Two-Audience Writing Lens

*Daily Surprise · 2026-07-29*

## What it is

Splitglass is a self-contained bifocal editor for writing one note that must make sense to two audiences. Tap any phrase and mark it as part of the **shared backbone**, **Lens A only**, or **Lens B only**. Two synchronized previews immediately show the resulting readings. You can solo either version, inspect only the common core, or drag a comparison reveal between them.

A harmless fictional reading-room notice is loaded at launch, so the mark → compare → export loop is understandable immediately. Replace it with your own text when useful. Splitglass does not generate, rewrite, score, grade, or upload text; it only edits material already in the open tab.

## Privacy-safe inspiration

The generic inspiration was the challenge of coordinating one change across several work surfaces while keeping shared intent consistent and adapting presentation for different readers. Splitglass contains no real people, organizations, projects, accounts, conversations, messages, or datasets.

Everything stays in the open browser tab. There are no network requests, external assets, analytics, accounts, uploads, persistent storage, packages, or server.

## How to open

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No server, install, account, or network connection is required.

## Controls

1. Tap one or more phrases to select them; selected phrases turn dark.
2. Assign the selection to **Shared**, **Lens A**, or **Lens B**.
3. Use **Split phrase** on one selected phrase containing a comma or semicolon, or **Merge** adjacent selected phrases.
4. Switch among **Both**, **Solo A**, **Solo B**, **Backbone**, and **Compare** previews.
5. In Compare mode, drag the reveal handle; keyboard users can focus it and use arrow, Home, or End keys.
6. Use **Undo** or **Reset**, rename either lens, or load a short note of your own.
7. Copy or download either finished version, the common backbone, or all three in one text file.

Keyboard shortcuts in the phrase editor: Tab or arrow keys move between phrases, Space selects, 1 marks Shared, 2 marks Lens A, 3 marks Lens B, and Escape clears the selection.

## Why it was built

Many notes need a stable common truth but different amounts of explanation for different readers. Splitglass makes that structure tangible without asking an automated system to invent either version. The result is both a thinking aid and a practical exporter: you can see exactly what stays common, what changes, and what each reader receives.
